McBopomofo pronunciation data
Source: https://github.com/openvanilla/McBopomofo/tree/18e2060dd5d0b511753ad79a8fe0d112e944f1af/Source/Data

BPMFBase.txt: Han character readings. BPMFMappings.txt: readings for the 5,000 Chinese prompts.
Converted to numbered syllable tables, deduplicated, and supplemented with editorial prompt readings.
Compiled prompt selection and editorial additions: CC BY-SA 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
The prompt selection uses the existing word bank; see public/word-sources.json for its source notices.
The upstream Data/README.md identifies the phrase mappings as derived from libtabe tsi.src (BSD).
See libtabe-notice.txt for the retained upstream notices.
